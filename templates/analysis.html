DETECTION LOGIC

Transparent rule set

ACTIVE
R01

Contact frequency

Average messages per sender per observed day: low-frequency at 2 or fewer; medium-frequency above 2 through 5; high-frequency above 5.

VOLUME
R02

Repeated-contact pattern

A sender-to-receiver pair is marked when 3 or more messages from that pair appear in the loaded dataset. This is a dataset-wide count, not a statement about intent.

PAIR COUNT
R03

Increasing-frequency pattern

Compares average daily volume in the later half of the observed period with the earlier half. An increase of at least 50% with 4 or more messages adds an indicator.

TREND
R04

Cross-platform pattern

Marks a sender-receiver pair when its records appear on 2 or more distinct platform values.

CHANNELS
R05

Multiple-account contact pattern

Marks a sender when their records include 2 or more distinct receiver account labels. This says nothing about whether the accounts represent different people.

ACCOUNTS
R06

Attempts marked unanswered

Uses response_status values such as “unanswered” or “no response”. At least half of 2 or more records must be marked this way.
